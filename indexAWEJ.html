Three ways to build a soundtrack.

Whether you're directing a stage production, finishing a film, or somewhere in the middle — there's a way in. Pick the door that fits where you are.

Tailored compositions
for your project.

Original music written from the inside of your script, your edit, your room. From a single cue to a full score — composed, arranged, and produced for the work it serves.

In-house sound
design.

The world your story lives in — built note by note, foley by foley. I handle atmospheres, transitions, sound effects, and final mixes — all designed to sit underneath the language without ever crowding it.

Feedback &
revisions.

The first cue is rarely the final cue. We work in passes — review sessions, A/B comparisons, and revisions baked into the timeline. Together, the score grows up with the production.

Featured work.

Six recent productions where the music carried as much of the storytelling as the lights or the language. Each was written for the room it lives in.

A composer for stage and screen.

Music that listens to the work it's written for.

I write original music and design sound for theatre, film, and television — most recently for Sjællands Teatret, Folketeatret, Odense Teater, and Teater Zeppelin.

My practice is built around the script. I compose from the inside of the scene out: a score that knows when to lift the room, when to step back, and when to disappear entirely. I want music that feels less added-on than discovered — written in the rehearsal room, refined in the mix.
