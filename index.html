Alexander Wrede Elung

Composer and music producer based in Copenhagen.

Hear the work Undervisning & tabs Get in touch

My goal is to create music that adds emotion, character, and atmosphere while serving the project’s vision. Collaboration is central to my process.

No two works demand the same approach. Genre, mood, and instrumentation are shaped in response to the project’s needs, whether through modern electronic production and VST instruments, historically and culturally accurate instrumentation, or entirely original sonic textures.
